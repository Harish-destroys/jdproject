END-TO-END ARCHITECTURE

How SecondServe Orchestrates Every Rescue

From the moment surplus is identified in a banquet kitchen to the moment meals are served at a community shelter, every handoff is audited and verified.

STEP 1: THE DONOR

Surplus Listing in Minutes

Hotels and catering managers access their dedicated donor portal. They submit the food category, portions, preparation timestamp, safe storage parameters (e.g. insulated hot container >65°C), and allergen details.

  • Prevents food spoilage by calculating dynamic countdown timers.
  • Automatic digital food safety guarantee logged into database.
STEP 2: THE NGO

Instant Request & Capacity Matching

Verified charities, orphanages, and emergency kitchens browse active listings in their vicinity. With a single click, an NGO requests the donation, inputting beneficiary headcounts and target arrival window.

  • Race-condition prevention: Once requested, listing locks against duplicate claims.
  • Automatic SMS/Push alert sent straight to the donor chef.
STEP 3: THE VOLUNTEER

Smart Volunteer Dispatch & Custody Chain

Once the donor approves the NGO request, active volunteer couriers (with bikes, vans, or cargo carts) receive an alert. The courier verifies the hygiene seal, confirms pickup, and transports the cargo directly.

  • Live 7-step tracker visible to all participants.
  • Digital custody signature recorded at pickup and drop-off.
STEP 4: IMPACT VERIFICATION

Distribution Logged & Loop Closed

The receiving NGO confirms safe delivery, serves the meals to beneficiaries, and marks the cycle as "Distribution Completed". Feedback and metric tallies update automatically across platform dashboards.

The 7 Strict Lifecycle Statuses
1. Listed: Added by Donor with expiry window.
2. Requested: NGO claims batch with beneficiary count.
3. Approved: Donor confirms packaging ready for pickup.
4. Volunteer Assigned: Courier accepts the transport route.
5. Picked Up: Temperature checked and in transit.
6. Delivered: Received by NGO shelter kitchen.
7. Distribution Completed: Meals served & verified.
